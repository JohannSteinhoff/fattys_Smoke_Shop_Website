Our Story

A San Marcos staple since 2007

Fatty's Smoke Shop has been serving San Marcos and the Texas State community for over 15 years. What started as a neighborhood tobacco shop has grown into a one-stop destination for everything from premium glass to the latest disposables.

We're known for a diverse inventory, fair pricing, frequent in-store promotions, and a friendly crew that's happy to point you to the right product — whether you're a first-timer or a longtime regular.
